Token FAQ

Token questions.

What is TRCR?

TRCR is Tracer's governance token on Base. Holders use it to propose and vote on how the Carrot protocol runs.

What is the official contract address?

It is published on the token page at launch, and nowhere else. Always check it before you buy.

Where can I buy TRCR?

On QuickSwap, on Base. Use the link on our token page so you land on the official pair.

What is the total supply?

12,500,000,000 TRCR to be confirmed, all minted once at genesis. There is no mint function, so supply can never go up; burns can only bring it down.

Where are the tokens held?

In five Safes: Tracer Foundation, Team & advisors, Ecosystem, Treasury and Early backers. Their addresses are published on the token page.

Is TRCR an investment?

TRCR gives you a vote in the Tracer DAO. It is not a share in a company and promises no return. Crypto assets are volatile; read the risk disclaimer.

Who gets the airdrop?

Eligibility will be published on the token page before the drop to be confirmed.

Do I need to claim the airdrop?

How tokens arrive will be published on the token page to be confirmed. We will never ask for your recovery phrase or send you to another site to claim.

Someone sent me a DM about the airdrop. Is it real?

No. The team never sends the first message. Only trust the links on this site.

Still stuck?
